Physical Studio Goods

Nationwide Shipping Across the United States

All non-perishable studio creations ship across the United States. This includes our flagship publications, physical bakeshop tools, and archival art pieces:

✦ Publications & Decks Bake to Baseline Guided Journals and The Deconstructed Recipe Decks.
✦ Archival DIY Studio Kits Boxed kits with wooden 3D forms, canvas panels, whipped spackle medium, quartz crystals, and mineral pigments.
✦ Original Art Pieces Framed 2D confectionery canvases and 3D keepsake cake sculptures finished on gold presentation drums.
✦ Remote Workshop Kits Tier 3 individual kits shipped directly to remote team members for live virtual sessions.
  • ✦ Carriers: We ship via USPS Priority Mail and UPS Ground to ensure packages arrive promptly and safely.
  • ✦ Processing Time: Standard shoppe orders are prepared by hand and dispatched within 3 to 5 business days.
  • ✦ Doorstep Tracking: An automated confirmation email with tracking details is sent as soon as your shipping label is printed.
  • ✦ Protective Packaging: Fragile items like 3D sculptures and pressed botanicals are cushioned with custom padding, protective wraps, and heavy-duty outer boxes.
Perishable & Fresh Bakes

Local Pickup, DFW Delivery & Cottage Food Rules

Texas Cottage Food Law Disclosure

This food is made in a home kitchen and is not inspected by the Department of State Health Services or a local health authority.

Edible products are prepared in a private residential kitchen that handles common allergens including wheat, dairy, eggs, soy, and nuts. Keep perishable food refrigerated until consumption.

Under Texas Cottage Food Law, fresh edible items cannot be shipped through parcel carriers across state lines. Fresh food items are available exclusively for local pickup in McKinney, Texas, or scheduled hand-delivery throughout Collin County and the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex.

This applies specifically to Fresh Edible Edition Studio Kits (pre-baked mini cakes and fresh buttercreams) and in-person workshop tracks. When placing an edible order, please specify your preferred pickup window or delivery address at checkout.

Electronic Guides

Digital Guides & Downloads

Resources marked as digital downloads (such as our free starter guide, printable frameworks, or PDF worksheets) are delivered immediately via email upon submission or checkout.

Please double check that your email address is spelled correctly. If your download link does not appear in your inbox within 15 minutes, please check your spam or promotions folder before reaching out to us.

Session Attendance

Workshop Reservations & Rescheduling

Tier 1: Public Studio Seats

Table seating for our public studio sessions in McKinney is kept intentionally small to maintain a calm, sensory-friendly atmosphere. Because seats are limited, reservations are non-refundable. However, if you let us know at least 48 hours before your session, we are happy to transfer your seat to a future open date or issue a studio credit.

Tier 2 & Tier 3: Private Local and Remote Virtual Sessions

Private team sessions and group retreats require a deposit to hold your date. Final headcount and remote shipping addresses must be locked at least 14 days before the event date so kits can be assembled and shipped on time.

Customer Care

Returns, Refunds & Damaged Items

Physical Shoppe Goods (Journals, Recipe Decks, Unopened Kits)

Unused physical items in their original, unopened packaging may be returned within 14 days of delivery. Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer. Please email us before sending any items back so we can provide the correct return address and confirm your return.

Custom Commissions & Heirloom Recipe Decks

Original handcrafted art pieces and custom family heirloom recipe decks are individually made to personal specifications. Because of this personalized craftsmanship, all sales on custom artwork and personalized decks are final once design work or painting begins.

Digital Downloads & Workshop Seats

Due to the instant delivery of digital files and limited workshop table space, sales on digital PDF products and live session seats are non-refundable.

Items Damaged in Transit

If your package arrives with shipping damage, please take photos of the box and damaged contents right away and send them to contact@thegildedspoonshoppe.com within 48 hours of delivery. We will make it right with a quick replacement or solution.
