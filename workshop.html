The 5-Layer Framework™

A traditional recipe demands flawless precision, which triggers executive fatigue. Our Framework breaks down your environment into five predictable, protective steps to help you safely regulate your nervous system.

1

The Invitation

(Color & Visual Signal)

Using 6-color spectrums to signal your nervous system—from calming sage/sky blue to energizing dopamine corals.

2

The Memory

(Aroma & Nostalgia)

Pairing culinary vanilla, roasted espresso, or aromatic spa oils to bypass analytical thought loops and tap into nostalgic safety.

3

The Anchor

(High-Friction Texture)

Deploying high-viscosity palette knife sweeps or coarse sugar/rock salt crunch to physically interrupt spinning overthinking loops.

4

The Strategy

(Placement & Boundaries)

Placing elements based on social battery: hidden internal pockets, radial mandalas for sharing, or protective shell-piped borders.

5

The Shift

(External Unmasking)

Externalizing chaotic feelings onto a 3D cake canvas so your mind is safe to rest, leaving you with a tangible keepsake or delicious reward.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do hybrid team bookings work?

Hybrid bookings are seamless! If you book an in-person Private Local Team Lab ($135/seat, meeting the 10-seat total minimum), you can add individual shipped kits ($145/kit + shipping) for your out-of-state or remote colleagues. Your remote members receive their 3D Sculpture kits at home and stream directly into the live session with your local office group.

Can a team book individual public seats instead of a private lab?

Yes! Small teams are welcome to reserve seats in our scheduled Public Studio Cohorts ($115/seat) if seats are open on our public calendar. However, teams who require date/time flexibility, a private venue room, on-site office setup, or tailored corporate debriefs should book a Private Local Team Lab ($135/seat, 10-seat total minimum).

Are shipped virtual corporate kits edible or non-edible?

Shipped virtual kits are exclusively non-edible 3D Keepsake Sculptures. To guarantee material quality and avoid food decay or transit risks, each shipped box contains a 6x4x2 cake foam block, 8x8 cake drum, spackle modeling paste, raw gemstones, piping tools, and dried botanicals for a permanent desk or home artwork.

What is the difference between the Edible and Non-Edible tracks?

The Non-Edible Sculpture Track uses a 6x4x2 foam cake block on an 8x8 cake drum, spackle paste, palette knives, 6 raw gemstones, and dried botanicals to create a permanent artwork. The Edible Track uses a real gourmet mini cake bar, tinted buttercreams, and culinary food gems (rock sugars, pink sea salt, espresso grounds, cocoa nibs) for a delicious creation.

How much is shipping for remote kits?

Shipped kits (Tier 3 virtual events & Tier 2 hybrid remote add-ons) incur a flat $12.00 ground shipping fee per kit anywhere in the contiguous United States, which includes door-to-door tracking for every team member. Bulk multi-kit shipments to a single corporate office location can be calculated at exact freight upon request.
