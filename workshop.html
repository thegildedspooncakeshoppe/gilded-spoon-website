The 5 Sensory Steps

Traditional classes can feel like a stressful exam. Our step-by-step approach gives your nervous system a structured, calming rhythm so you can focus on pure tactile enjoyment.

1

Visual Calm

(Color & Tone)

Choose soothing, coordinated color palettes that ease eye strain and create instant visual balance.

2

Comfort & Scent

(Aroma & Memory)

Gentle vanilla and subtle botanical notes quiet mental loops and evoke comforting memories.

3

Tactile Grounding

(Piping & Textures)

Rhythmic piping sweeps and smooth palette knife work pull focus out of your head and into your hands.

4

Gentle Boundary

(Composition)

Clear geometric guides and shell-piped borders ensure you always know exactly what to do next.

5

Creative Joy

(Your Keepsake)

Walk away with a finished art piece or edible treat you feel proud of—feeling recharged and unburdened.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do hybrid team workshops work?

Hybrid bookings are simple! If you book an in-person team session ($135/seat, meeting our 10-seat event minimum), you can add individual shipped kits ($145/kit + $12 ground shipping) for remote colleagues. They receive their 3D sculpture kits at home and stream directly into the live session with your local group.

What is the difference between Edible and 3D Sculpture tracks?

The 3D Keepsake Sculpture Track uses a wooden foam block, lightweight spackle paste, palette knives, gemstones, and botanicals to make a permanent desk or home art piece. The Edible Track uses freshly baked mini cakes, gourmet buttercreams, and confectionery gems for a delicious treat.

Are shipped corporate kits edible or non-edible?

Shipped virtual kits are exclusively non-edible 3D keepsake sculptures. This ensures all materials arrive in perfect condition without perishable food risks, giving participants a permanent decorative keepsake for their home or office.
