Our Studio Approach

The 5-Layer Sensory Method™

Most creative classes judge you on perfection. Our 5-layer framework is designed to help you decompress, one gentle layer at a time:

Layer 1

Color & Atmosphere

Choose a soothing, low-glare palette that immediately quiets visual noise and helps you feel settled.

Layer 2

Shape & Movement

Use piping bags and palette knives for rhythmic, repetitive motions that release mental tension and stress.

Layer 3

Texture & Touch

Run your fingertips along our texture bar to choose the exact tactile resistance—from smooth whipped paste to raw crystals—that your body wants today.

Layer 4

Boundaries & Space

Follow simple visual guidelines and shell-piped borders so you never have to wonder what to do next.

Layer 5

The Finishing Ritual

A conscious 30-second pause and a final flourish—like a splatter of gold luster or a delicate blossom—that declares your piece complete.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do hybrid team bookings work?

Hybrid bookings are simple! If you book an in-person Private Local Team Lab ($135/seat, meeting our 10-seat event minimum), you can add individual shipped kits ($145/kit + $12 ground shipping) for remote colleagues. They receive their keepsake sculpture kits at home and stream directly into the live session with your local group.

What is the difference between Edible and Keepsake Sculpture tracks?

The Keepsake Cake Sculpture Track uses a wooden art block, lightweight spackle paste, palette knives, gemstones, and botanicals to make a permanent desk or home art piece. The Edible Track uses freshly baked mini cakes, gourmet buttercreams, and confectionery gems for a delicious treat.

Are shipped virtual corporate kits edible or non-edible?

Shipped virtual kits are exclusively non-edible keepsake sculptures. This ensures all materials arrive in perfect condition without perishable food risks, giving participants a permanent decorative keepsake for their home or office.
