The 5-Layer Framework™

A traditional recipe demands flawless precision, which triggers executive fatigue. Our Framework breaks down your environment into five predictable, protective steps to help you safely regulate your nervous system.

1

The Invitation

(The Signal)

Using color to send a signal to your spirit—from the loud "Hello!" of yellow to the quiet "Hush" of lavender.

2

The Memory

(Shape & Flavor)

Flavor is a time machine. Choosing the taste and narrative that matches your story, taking you to safety or a bright future.

3

The Anchor

(The Texture)

Deploying texture—from a Crunch that snaps you out of a spiral to Velvet cream that soothes—to ground you in the present.

4

The Strategy

(The Placement)

Placing elements based on social battery: hidden internally for vulnerability, radial for sharing, or border edges for safety.

5

The Magic

(The Shift)

Checking in after the last bite: feeling the heaviness lift and the chaos quiet down—the ultimate shift of baking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior baking or design experience?

Not at all. There is no baking required during the sessions. All edible mini cakes or gallery canvas materials arrive pre-prepared so you skip kitchen cleanup and jump straight into sensory flow.

What do I take home from the laboratory session?

Depending on your lab track, you take home your custom-decorated mini cake canvas, a permanent gallery-wrapped modeling paste canvas, or physical Atelier Recipe Card Decks.

How are groups structured for sensory protection?

Seats are strictly capped for an intimate, low-noise environment. There are no harsh overhead lights or performance pressures.

Can I book a private cohort workshop or corporate wellness event?

Yes! We host private cohorts for families, business teams, ERGs, and neuroinclusive groups locally in-person or virtually with shipped kits. Use our contact page to request a private team proposal.
