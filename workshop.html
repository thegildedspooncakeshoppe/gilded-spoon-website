The 5-Layer Framework™

A traditional recipe demands flawless precision, which triggers executive fatigue. Our Framework breaks down your environment into five predictable, protective steps to help you safely regulate your nervous system.

1

The Invitation

(Color & Visual Signal)

Using color frequencies to signal your nervous system—from calming sage/sky blue to energizing dopamine corals.

2

The Memory

(Shape & Flavor)

Pairing zests or warm spices to bypass analytical thought loops and tap into nostalgic safety.

3

The Anchor

(High-Friction Texture)

Deploying high-viscosity paste swipes or jaw-resistance crunch to physically interrupt spinning overthinking loops.

4

The Strategy

(Placement & Boundaries)

Placing elements based on social battery: hidden internally, radial for sharing, or protective border edges.

5

The Shift

(External Unmasking)

Externalizing chaotic feelings onto a 3D canvas so your mind is safe to rest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior baking or art experience?

Not at all. There is no oven baking during the sessions. All 8x10 canvas panels, texture pastes, pre-baked mini cake foundations, and botanical accoutrements arrive pre-prepared so you skip kitchen cleanup and jump straight into sensory flow.

How do shipped remote corporate team workshops work?

We ship identical 3D Sensory Canvas Kits directly to remote employees' homes nationwide. Each kit includes an 8x10 canvas panel, plain spackle paste, a 6-pod Somatic Color Strip, piping bags, spatulas, and dried botanicals for a 60-minute virtual session.

What are the 6-Color Somatic Spectrums?

Our color strips are curated into three distinct themes—Sanctuary (Calming & Shielding), Grounding (Comfort & Earth Tones), and Dopamine (Awakening & Energy). Because all six shades in a strip belong to the same color family, participants can swirl and layer paste without making a muddy mess.

Where can I find your Recipe Deconstruction tools?

Recipe Deconstruction is offered exclusively as a physical and digital e-commerce product line. You can purchase our pre-deconstructed masterclass recipe decks (like the 33-card Lemon Raspberry set) and Zero-Math Bifold Conversion Guides directly in our Shoppe.
