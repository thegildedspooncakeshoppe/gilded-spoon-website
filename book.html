Dear Creative Friend,

If you are reading this with tense shoulders or an exhausted mind, take a deep breath.

For 20 years working as a nurse, I saw firsthand how fast burnout drains us. For years, I treated baking like another high-pressure exam—obsessing over perfect symmetry, smooth icing, and exact timing.

Then I realized: The calm isn't in a flawless dessert. It's in the soothing, rhythmic motion of making.

I created this guided journal so you have a safe, quiet space to put your thoughts that never demands perfection. Consider this your official permission to rest.

- Dimple
