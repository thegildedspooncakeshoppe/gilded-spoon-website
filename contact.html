Atelier Inquiries & Commissions

Whether reserving an individual workshop seat, requesting a private team proposal, commissioning a custom recipe deck, or stocking our shoppe tools, we welcome your message.

Workshop Inquiry Checklist

To help us tailor your experience or prepare an accurate team proposal, feel free to include:

  • Format & Location: In-person (McKinney, TX / your private venue) OR remote shipped kits.
  • Group Size & Type: Estimated headcount (e.g., individual seat, social gathering, corporate team, or ERG).
  • Lab Track Preference: Lab 01 (Somatic/Sensory Mini Cake or Canvas) OR Lab 02 (Cognitive Recipe Deconstruction).
  • Sensory & Dietary Notes: Specific allergen needs (eggless/GF) or low-stimulus environment requests.
Custom Recipe Deck Checklist

To help us design your low-overwhelm Somatic Recipe Deck, please feel free to include:

  • Recipe Name & Origin: Family heirloom, favorite signature bake, or specific pastry type.
  • Dietary/Sensory Needs: Explicit adaptations needed (e.g., eggless substitutions, gluten-free, dairy-free).
  • Special Cues: Specific step-by-step pain points or temperature checkpoints where executive fatigue usually sets in.
Wholesale & Retail Inquiry Checklist

Interested in stocking our sensory publications and card decks in your shop or wellness space? Feel free to share:

  • Shop Name & Location: Store name, city/state, and website or social media handle.
  • Store Concept: Independent bookstore, gift shop, wellness studio, museum shop, or online store.
  • Items of Interest: Bake to Baseline Guided Journals, 5-Layer Sensory Decks, or Somatic Recipe Decks.
  • Estimated Order Size / Timeline: Projected opening order quantity or target shelf launch date.
