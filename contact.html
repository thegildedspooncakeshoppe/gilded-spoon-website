Custom Recipe Deck Checklist

To help us design your low-overwhelm Somatic Recipe Deck, please feel free to include the following in your message:

  • Recipe Name & Origin: Family heirloom, favorite signature bake, or specific pastry type.
  • Dietary/Sensory Needs: Explicit adaptations needed (e.g., eggless substitutions, gluten-free, dairy-free).
  • Special Cues: Specific step-by-step pain points or temperature checkpoints where executive fatigue usually sets in.
