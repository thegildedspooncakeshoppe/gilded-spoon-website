Custom Artwork Checklist

To help Dimple understand your space and vision, please share:

  • Format Preference: 3D sculpted cake keepsake on a gold display drum, or framed 2D floral relief canvas for wall display.
  • Color Palette & Feeling: Neutral earth tones, calm greens, sunny yellows, or tones that match your home sanctuary.
  • Special Inclusions: Specific pressed florals, raw quartz crystals, or personal meaningful accents.
  • Timing & Destination: Any target date and city for shipping or local McKinney pickup.
Custom Recipe Deck Checklist

To help us translate your favorite recipe into single-step counter cards with zero math:

  • Recipe Name & Origin: Family heirloom, favorite holiday cake, or special bake passed down through generations.
  • Dietary Adaptations: Eggless, gluten-free, dairy-free, or specific ingredient replacements needed.
  • Kitchen Stress Points: Steps where timing, temperature, or multitasking usually feels overwhelming.
Private Group Workshop Checklist

For private in-person gatherings, workplace retreats, or virtual team kits:

  • Event Format: Private Local In-Person, Hybrid (local + remote shipped kits), or 100% Virtual Shipped.
  • Medium Track: Keepsake 3D Sculptures (permanent desk display) or Fresh Edible Cake Art (mini cakes with buttercreams).
  • Estimated Group Size: Approximate headcount and location (office, private venue, or remote doors).
  • Preferred Dates: Desired month, weekday, or weekend timing.
Wholesale & Retail Checklist

Interested in stocking our guided journals, recipe decks, or studio kits in your shop?

  • Store Information: Shop name, location (city/state), and online store or social link.
  • Items of Interest: Bake to Baseline Guided Journals, The Deconstructed Recipe Decks, or Studio DIY Kits.
  • Target Quantity & Timeline: Opening order size or planned shelf launch date.
